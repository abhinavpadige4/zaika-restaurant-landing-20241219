Meet Our Master Chefs

The artisans behind our authentic flavors

Chef Rajesh Kumar

Executive Chef & Culinary Director

With over 20 years of experience in authentic Indian cuisine, Chef Rajesh specializes in North Indian dishes and has trained under master chefs in Delhi and Punjab. His passion for preserving traditional recipes while adding contemporary touches makes every dish a masterpiece.

Chef Priya Sharma

Head Chef - South Indian Cuisine

Chef Priya brings the rich flavors of South India to our kitchen with her expertise in dosas, idlis, and coastal specialties. Her innovative approach to traditional recipes has earned her multiple culinary awards across India.

Chef Ahmed Hassan

Chef de Partie - Tandoor Specialist

Master of the tandoor oven, Chef Ahmed specializes in breads and grilled meats. His expertise in temperature control and spice blending ensures perfectly cooked naans, rotis, and tandoori dishes every time.
